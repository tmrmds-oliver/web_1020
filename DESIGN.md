# TMR Brand Site Design Notes

## Direction

Professional AI advisory, adapted from the existing single-page reference. The page should feel like a well-prepared executive workshop handout used in a bright Taipei meeting room: confident, calm, and practical.

## Color

Committed palette: deep ink blue, warm paper white, mineral green, and restrained signal orange for calls to action. Avoid pure black and pure white.

## Typography

Use Noto Sans TC for Chinese body text and Manrope for English labels/numerals. Large headings are compact and decisive; body copy remains spacious and readable.

## Layout

Use a clear 12-column desktop rhythm, generous vertical intervals, numbered process sections, and varied modules instead of repeated cards. Mobile stacks naturally with persistent action visibility.

## Motion

Short opacity and transform reveals only. Honor reduced-motion preferences. No decorative loops or parallax.
